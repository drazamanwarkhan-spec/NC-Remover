DSU Plan of Study & Student Course Audit System

Checks student enrolment/offering data against the applicable Plan of Study and flags courses outside the plan, and plan courses that were not offered (NC).

Upload Student Enrollment / Offering File
Excel supported: .xls and .xlsx. The app automatically detects Registration Number, Student Name, Program, Class/Section, Course Code, Course Title, Credit Hours, Semester and Academic Status. CSV/TSV also supported.
Upload Result / Grade Sheet (PSRN)
Accepts the university's Provisional Semester Result Notification as PDF, or an Excel/CSV export with Registration Number, Course Code, Grade, SGPA, CGPA, Credit Hours Completed and Remarks columns. No PSRN is preloaded. Upload the PSRN PDF/Excel/CSV only when you want to use result data.
Data Scope: Only students whose uploaded Program/Degree is recognized as BBA, BS-AF, BS-BAP or BS-FinTech are displayed. All other and blank/unknown programs are excluded at import. Students from all other programs are automatically excluded from the audit.

Cohort tagging: BBA212, BBA221, BBA222 and BS-AF 212, 221, 222 are explicitly assigned to the 2021 Roadmap.

Series / Plan Rules: For BBA, BS-BAP and BS-AF — series 231, 232, 241 use the 2023 Plan of Study; series 242, 251, 252 use the 2025 Plan of Study; series 261 onward use the Fall 2026 Plan of Study. BS-FinTech starts from FIN261 and follows the Fall 2026 Plan of Study for all series. Only courses found in the uploaded enrollment/offering data are displayed in the "Offered Courses" table. Each offered course is checked against the student's applicable Plan of Study using Course ID (and Course Name as a fallback) as the matching key. IN PLAN = the offered course is found in the applicable plan; ELECTIVE SLOT = it fills an elective placeholder for that semester; ELECTIVE (POOL) = it matches a course from the program's official elective pool (per the Annex electives list) even though it was offered in a different semester than the plan's generic Elective-I/II slot; NOT IN PLAN = the offered course is not part of the applicable plan and not in its elective pool. A second table, Plan Courses Not Offered (NC), lists any required (non-elective) course from the Plan of Study for a semester the student has data in, that was not found among the uploaded offered courses for that semester — these are marked NC. Electives are never marked NC since they are optional; a separate Electives Not Offered tab instead lists, informationally, which pool electives were not offered/taken by anyone currently in view.

Legacy series (before 231): cohorts such as 201/211/212/221/222 pre-date the 2023 Plan of Study documents loaded into this app. They're automatically matched to the closest available reference — the 2023 Plan — so the audit still runs, but treat NOT-IN-PLAN / NC results for these series as indicative rather than exact.
